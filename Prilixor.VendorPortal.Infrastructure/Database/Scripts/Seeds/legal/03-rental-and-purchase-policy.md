# Rental & Purchase Policy

**Effective Date:** September 8, 2026

This Policy explains how renting and buying equipment works on BlinksMed, and is incorporated into the Terms of Use.

## 1. Renting Equipment

1.1 **Tenure:** Each listing displays available rental durations (e.g., weekly/monthly) and the applicable per-period rate, shown before checkout as required under Rule 5, E-Commerce Rules, 2020.

1.2 **Security Deposit:** A refundable deposit is collected at booking and held in escrow. The deposit amount varies by product and product category and is displayed on each individual listing prior to checkout, in compliance with Rule 5, Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. It is not a rental fee and does not adjust against rent unless you elect to convert rental into purchase (see Section 3).

1.3 **Prescription Verification:** For regulated equipment (see Terms of Use, Section 3), your order will not be confirmed until a valid prescription is uploaded and verified. This may take up to 24 hours.

1.4 **Extension:** Rentals may be extended via the app subject to availability, at the then-applicable rate; unextended equipment must be returned by the tenure end date.

1.5 **Condition at Handover:** A joint digital condition report (with photos) is generated at delivery. You must review and flag discrepancies within 24 hours of delivery, failing which the Vendor's report is treated as accepted.

1.6 **Return:** Equipment must be returned in the condition received (ordinary wear and tear excepted), fully sanitised, with all original accessories. Pickup can be scheduled via the app.

1.7 **Late Return:** A late fee equal to 10% of the product's listed rental value per day applies for each day beyond the agreed tenure, disclosed at booking.

1.8 **Deposit Refund:** Refundable within 7 business days of verified return, subject to deduction for:
 (a) damage beyond normal wear and tear, assessed against the handover condition report;
 (b) missing accessories/parts;
 (c) outstanding late fees.
 Any deduction will be itemised and communicated to you with photographic evidence before final settlement.

## 2. Buying Equipment

2.1 Purchases are final sales subject to the Vendor's stated warranty (if any) and the Cancellation & Refund Policy for defective/wrong items.

2.2 All purchase listings display MRP, GST, and (for refurbished items) the refurbishment/condition disclosure, in line with the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.

2.3 A GST-compliant invoice is issued by the Vendor for every purchase.

## 3. Rent-to-Own Conversion (where offered)

Where a listing offers "convert to purchase," a pro-rata portion of prior rental payments may be adjusted against the purchase price as displayed on that specific listing at the time of conversion. Terms vary by Vendor and are shown before you confirm conversion.

## 4. Equipment Malfunction During Rental

4.1 If rented equipment malfunctions through no fault of yours, report it via the app within 24 hours. BlinksMed/the Vendor will arrange repair, replacement, or pro-rata refund of the unused rental period, at the User's choice, within 3 business days.

4.2 If malfunction is found to result from misuse, no refund/replacement obligation arises, and repair costs may be deducted from the deposit, with evidence provided to you.

## 5. Hygiene & Safety Disclosure

All equipment must be sanitised by the Vendor before each dispatch, per BlinksMed's Vendor hygiene standards. Users may request the sanitisation certificate for any order.
