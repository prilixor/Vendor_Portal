# Vendor / Seller Policy

**Effective Date:** September 8, 2026

This Vendor Policy governs the relationship between BlinksMed and third-party vendors ("Vendor", "Seller") who list medical/healthcare equipment for rent or sale on the Platform. It supplements the Vendor Onboarding Agreement signed at registration.

## 1. Onboarding & Verification

1.1 Vendors must provide, prior to activation: GST registration (where applicable), PAN, bank account details, business address proof, and — for equipment classified as medical devices under the Medical Devices Rules, 2017 — relevant manufacturing/import/wholesale licence details (e.g., CDSCO registration where required).

1.2 BlinksMed reserves the right to conduct due diligence, request physical/photographic verification of inventory condition, and reject or suspend any Vendor at its discretion for non-compliance.

## 2. Listing Standards

2.1 Vendors are solely responsible for the accuracy of: product title, condition (new/refurbished/pre-owned), images, functional specifications, sanitisation/disinfection status, MRP and rental rate, security deposit amount, and manufacturer/importer details, in compliance with the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and Consumer Protection Act, 2019 Section 2(47) (unfair trade practice — false representation).

2.2 Vendors must not list expired-warranty, recalled, or unsafe equipment, and must disclose known defects. Deliberate misrepresentation will result in immediate delisting and may be reported to relevant authorities.

2.3 For rental listings, Vendors must provide a written condition report and sanitisation certificate at each handover, which becomes the baseline for deposit-deduction disputes.

## 3. Pricing & Commission

3.1 Vendors set their own rental/sale price and deposit amount, subject to BlinksMed's commission structure disclosed in the Vendor Onboarding Agreement, deducted at settlement.

3.2 Vendors may not engage in price manipulation, bait pricing, or artificially inflate MRP to show fake discounts (Rule 5(3), E-Commerce Rules, 2020).

## 4. Order Fulfilment & Logistics

4.1 Vendors must confirm order acceptance within 24 hours, and hand over equipment in verified working, sanitised condition within the delivery window committed at checkout.

4.2 Where BlinksMed's logistics partner is used, Vendor must package equipment per the partner's guidelines. Where Vendor self-fulfils, Vendor bears responsibility for timely, safe delivery and pickup.

## 5. Returns, Deposit Disputes & Damage Assessment

5.1 On equipment return, the Vendor must inspect against the original condition report within 48 hours and report any damage/missing parts via the Platform, with photographic evidence, before any deposit deduction is approved.

5.2 Disputed deductions are adjudicated by BlinksMed's grievance team; the Vendor agrees to be bound by this determination as a condition of using the Platform, without prejudice to either party's right to pursue civil remedies.

## 6. Vendor Obligations Under Consumer Law

Vendors, as "sellers" under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, independently remain liable for:
- Defects in goods/services and any resulting consumer complaint or claim before a Consumer Commission;
- Providing GST-compliant invoices for each transaction;
- Honouring the cancellation/refund timelines disclosed at checkout;
- Cooperating with BlinksMed's grievance redressal process and providing information within 48 hours of a request.

## 7. Suspension & Termination

BlinksMed may suspend or terminate a Vendor's account for: repeated valid consumer complaints, failure to maintain hygiene/safety standards, non-payment of commission, fraud, or breach of applicable law. Outstanding consumer obligations survive termination.

## 8. Indemnity

Vendor indemnifies BlinksMed against any claim, penalty, or loss arising from Vendor's inaccurate listings, defective/unsafe equipment, or breach of applicable law, including the Medical Devices Rules, 2017, Legal Metrology Act, 2009, and Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
