# Cancellation & Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** September 8, 2026

This Policy is disclosed at checkout in compliance with Rule 5(4), Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

## 1. Order Cancellation by User

| Stage | Rental Orders | Purchase Orders |
|---|---|---|
| Before Vendor confirms | Full refund, no charge | Full refund, no charge |
| After confirmation, before dispatch | 10% cancellation fee | 10% cancellation fee |
| After dispatch/delivery | Not cancellable; return process applies | Not cancellable; return process applies |

## 2. Order Cancellation by Vendor/BlinksMed

If a Vendor cancels after confirmation (e.g., stock unavailability, failed quality check), you receive a **full refund** including any cancellation fee already charged, within 5–7 business days, plus priority rebooking assistance where feasible.

## 3. Returns & Refunds — Purchased Items

3.1 You may request a return within 7 days of delivery if the item is:
 (a) materially different from the listing description;
 (b) defective or damaged in transit;
 (c) missing parts/accessories.

3.2 Return requests must include photos/video of the issue. Approved returns are picked up within 3 business days; refund is processed within 5–7 business days of the Vendor confirming receipt and condition of the returned item.

3.3 Change-of-mind returns are accepted only where the specific listing states so, given hygiene considerations applicable to medical equipment.

## 4. Refunds — Rentals

See Rental & Purchase Policy Section 1.8 for deposit refund timelines and permissible deductions.

## 5. Refund Method

Refunds are credited to the original payment method. Where the original method is unavailable, refund is issued to a verified bank account/UPI ID within the same timelines.

## 6. Non-Refundable Items/Charges

- One-time sanitisation/logistics fee (if separately charged and disclosed at checkout) is non-refundable once dispatch has occurred, except where the order is cancelled due to Vendor/BlinksMed fault.
- Payment gateway charges are non-refundable where the transaction itself is not reversed by the gateway.

## 7. Disputes on Refund Amount

If you disagree with a refund/deduction amount, raise it with the Grievance Officer within 7 days of the deduction; see Grievance Redressal Policy for the resolution process and timelines.
