# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy is issued in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

## 1. Personal Data We Collect

- **Identity & contact data:** name, phone, email, delivery address.
- **Health-adjacent data:** prescriptions/medical justification uploaded for regulated equipment (treated as sensitive personal data).
- **Payment data:** processed via PCI-DSS-compliant gateway partners; BlinksMed does not store full card numbers.
- **Usage data:** device ID, IP address, app interaction logs, location (if permitted), for fraud prevention and service improvement.

## 2. Purpose of Processing (DPDP Act, Section 4 — purpose limitation)

We process your data only for: account creation and order fulfilment, prescription verification for regulated equipment, payment and deposit processing, delivery/logistics coordination, grievance redressal, fraud/abuse prevention, and legally mandated record-keeping (e.g., GST invoicing).

## 3. Consent

3.1 By registering, you provide consent under Section 6 of the DPDP Act for the above purposes. Consent for uploading prescription/health-related documents is taken separately and explicitly at the relevant step.

3.2 You may withdraw consent at any time via Account Settings → Privacy → Manage Consent, or by writing to our Data Protection contact; withdrawal will not affect processing already carried out, and may result in inability to complete active orders.

## 4. Sharing of Data

4.1 We share necessary order data (name, address, phone, prescription where applicable) with the relevant Vendor solely to fulfil your order, and with logistics partners solely for delivery/pickup.

4.2 We do not sell personal data to third parties. Data may be disclosed where required by law, court order, or a competent government authority under Section 7(b)–(c) of the DPDP Act.

4.3 Vendors and logistics partners are contractually bound to use your data only for order fulfilment and not for independent marketing.

## 5. Data Retention

Personal data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as mandated under applicable law (e.g., GST/Income Tax record-keeping — typically 8 years for financial records). Prescription documents are retained only for the duration required for the specific order plus 90 days, then securely deleted, unless you consent to longer retention for repeat-order convenience.

## 6. Your Rights (DPDP Act, Sections 11–14)

You have the right to: access a summary of your personal data and processing activities; request correction/updating of inaccurate data; request erasure of data no longer necessary for the stated purpose; nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death/incapacity; and file a grievance with our Data Protection contact, and thereafter with the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved.

## 7. Security

We implement reasonable security practices under the IT Rules, 2011, including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular security reviews. In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify affected Users and the Data Protection Board as required under Section 8(6) of the DPDP Act.

## 8. Children's Data

The Platform is not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if discovered, such data will be deleted.

## 9. Cookies

We use cookies/similar technologies for session management, analytics, and fraud prevention. You can control cookie preferences via your browser settings.
