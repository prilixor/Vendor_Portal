## 2. Process & Timelines

1. **Acknowledgement:** Every complaint (via email, in-app support, or phone) is acknowledged within **48 hours**.
2. **Resolution:** Complaints are resolved within **one month** from the date of receipt, as mandated under Rule 4(5), E-Commerce Rules 2020. Deposit/refund disputes and equipment-safety complaints are prioritised and typically resolved within 7–10 days.
3. **Escalation:** If unresolved or unsatisfactorily resolved, you may escalate, marking the subject line "ESCALATION," to parth@blinksmed.com / +91 8153905390, which will be treated as a Level-2 escalation and prioritised for review by the founding team.

## 3. What You Can Complain About

- Deposit deduction disputes
- Delayed/failed refunds
- Defective, unsafe, or unsanitised equipment
- Vendor misconduct or misrepresentation
- Delivery/pickup failures
- Data privacy concerns

## 4. External Remedies (Your Statutory Rights)

If not satisfied with BlinksMed's resolution, you may approach:
- **National Consumer Helpline:** 1915, or the UMANG app
- **e-Daakhil Portal:** edaakhil.nic.in (for filing consumer complaints online with District/State/National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions)
- **Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA)**, for unfair trade practices or false/misleading advertisements
- **Data Protection Board of India**, for unresolved personal data grievances under the DPDP Act, 2023

Nothing in this Policy limits your right to directly approach a Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission having jurisdiction, including the Commission local to your residence, as provided under Section 34(2) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

## 5. Record-Keeping

BlinksMed maintains a log of all complaints, acknowledgement dates, and resolution dates for a minimum of 3 years for regulatory audit purposes.
