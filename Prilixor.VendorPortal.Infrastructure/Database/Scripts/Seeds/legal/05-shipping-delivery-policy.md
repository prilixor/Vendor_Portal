# Shipping, Delivery & Pickup Policy

**Effective Date:** September 8, 2026

## 1. Serviceable Areas

BlinksMed currently delivers/picks up in the following cities/pincodes: Ahmedabad and Rajkot, along with adjoining serviceable pincodes within these regions. Serviceability is checked at checkout via pincode.

## 2. Delivery Timelines

Estimated delivery windows are shown per-listing at checkout (e.g., "same-day," "next-day," "2–3 business days") depending on Vendor location and equipment type, as required under Rule 5, E-Commerce Rules, 2020.

## 3. Delivery Charges

Delivery/logistics charges (if any) are displayed at checkout before payment and are not added post-purchase.

## 4. Installation & Demonstration

For equipment requiring setup (e.g., oxygen concentrators, hospital beds), Vendor delivery personnel will provide basic installation and a usage walkthrough at handover, where indicated on the listing.

## 5. Failed Delivery

If delivery fails due to an incorrect address or unavailability of the recipient after 2 delivery attempts, the order may be cancelled per the Cancellation & Refund Policy, with delivery charges (if incurred) deducted from the refund.

## 6. Pickup for Rental Returns

Pickup is scheduled through the app; you will receive an OTP-based pickup confirmation. Please ensure equipment is packed as received. Vendor/logistics partner will conduct the condition check at pickup itself where feasible.

## 7. Damage in Transit

Report transit damage within 24 hours of delivery with photos; BlinksMed will coordinate replacement or refund per the Cancellation & Refund Policy without treating it as a User-caused deposit deduction.
